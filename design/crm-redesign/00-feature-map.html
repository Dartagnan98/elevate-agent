What I'd build first

The daily-use core is Section 01 and 02 — and most of it is already sitting in your database waiting for a proper screen. My recommendation for the first mockup:

  1. The contact card — profile up top, threaded notes, and the unified text-and-email timeline in one view.
  2. The compose box — with the text ↔ email toggle and template picker built in.
  3. The "today" list — who's hot and who's owed a follow-up, one click into their card.
